## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Alternative contact methods
The system SHALL show the alternative support contact methods as a 2-column grid of cards, each with an icon, a title and a description.

#### Scenario: Four contact cards
- **WHEN** a user opens the contacts screen
- **THEN** four cards are shown: WhatsApp (MessageCircle icon, "Fale pelo WhatsApp"), Email (Mail icon, "Envie um email"), Telefone (Phone icon, "Ligue para nós") and Horário de atendimento (Clock icon)
